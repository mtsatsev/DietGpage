# 5-Day Athletic Diet Plan

A practical nutrition plan for a highly active male athlete training 1–2 times per day.

## Athlete Profile

| Characteristic | Value |
|---|---:|
| Age | 27 |
| Height | 187 cm |
| Weight | ~80 kg |
| Training level | Highly athletic |
| Supplements | Whey protein, creatine |

## Weekly Training Schedule

| Day | Training |
|---|---|
| Monday | Morning calisthenics + Muay Thai |
| Tuesday | Morning calisthenics + Volleyball |
| Wednesday | Morning calisthenics + Muay Thai |
| Thursday | Morning calisthenics + Volleyball |
| Friday | Morning calisthenics + Muay Thai |
| Saturday | Morning calisthenics + Muay Thai conditioning |
| Sunday | Rest day |

### Morning Baseline Training

Most days start with:

- 100 pull-ups
- 200 push-ups
- 300 bodyweight squats

Sometimes this is replaced by an actual gym session.

## Nutrition Targets

These are starting estimates. Adjust based on bodyweight, recovery, appetite, and performance.

| Day Type | Calories | Protein | Carbs | Fat |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Muay Thai / double-session day | 3,500–3,900 kcal | 160–180 g | 450–550 g | 80–100 g |
| Volleyball / moderate day | 3,200–3,600 kcal | 155–175 g | 380–480 g | 85–105 g |
| Conditioning day | 3,400–3,800 kcal | 160–180 g | 420–520 g | 85–100 g |
| Rest day | 2,800–3,100 kcal | 160–180 g | 250–350 g | 90–110 g |

## Supplement Baseline

### Creatine

Take **3–5 g creatine monohydrate daily**, with any meal or shake.

### Whey Protein

Use whey to fill protein gaps, not as a replacement for real meals.

- **1 scoop** on normal training days
- **2 scoops** on heavy, double-session, gym, or rushed-meal days

### Hydration

- Drink at least **3–4 L water per day**
- Increase intake on Muay Thai and conditioning days
- Add electrolytes or salt when sweating heavily

## Meal Timing Rules

| Timing | Goal |
|---|---|
| Before early calisthenics | Light carbs, hydration, salt |
| After calisthenics | Full breakfast with protein + carbs |
| 2–3 hours before evening training | Carb-heavy meal, moderate protein, lower fat |
| 30–60 minutes before training | Fast carbs if needed |
| After training | Protein + carbs + fluids + salt |

### Post-Training Target

After hard training, aim for:

- 30–45 g protein
- 80–120 g carbs
- Fluids and electrolytes

# Day 1 — Muay Thai Double-Session Day

Use for Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

**Target:** 3,600–3,900 kcal

## Early Morning — Before Calisthenics

Choose one:

### Option A

- 1 banana
- Coffee or espresso
- Water with a pinch of salt

### Option B

- 2 rice cakes
- Honey
- Water

## Breakfast — After Calisthenics

- 100 g oats
- 300 ml milk or soy milk
- 1 scoop whey protein
- 1 banana or berries
- 20 g peanut butter
- 2 whole eggs

Approximate target: **850–950 kcal**, **55–65 g protein**

## Lunch

- 180–220 g chicken breast, turkey, or lean beef
- 300–400 g cooked rice or potatoes
- Big salad or vegetables
- 1–2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 fruit

Approximate target: **850–1,000 kcal**, **55–65 g protein**

## Pre-Muay Thai Meal — 2–3 Hours Before Training

Keep it carb-heavy, moderate protein, and not too fatty.

Example:

- 120 g dry pasta
- 150 g chicken
- Tomato sauce
- Small amount of parmesan
- Fruit or fruit juice

Approximate target: **750–900 kcal**

## 30–60 Minutes Before Training

Choose one:

- Banana
- Dates
- Honey sandwich
- Sports drink
- Rice cakes with honey

Add electrolytes if sweating heavily.

## Dinner — After Muay Thai

- 200 g salmon, beef, chicken, or tofu
- 350–500 g potatoes or cooked rice
- Vegetables
- Greek yogurt or cottage cheese

Approximate target: **900–1,100 kcal**

## Before Bed

- 250 g Greek yogurt, skyr, or cottage cheese
- Honey or berries
- Optional nuts

# Adjustment Rules

## If Weight Is Dropping or Performance Feels Flat

Add **300–500 kcal per day**, mostly from carbohydrates.

Easy additions:

- Add 100 g cooked rice twice per day
- Add 1 banana + honey
- Add 60–80 g oats to a shake
- Add extra pasta or potatoes at dinner

## If Gaining Fat Too Quickly

Remove **200–300 kcal per day**, mostly from fats or non-training carbs.

Easy reductions:

- Less peanut butter
- Fewer nuts
- Slightly less olive oil
- Smaller carb portions on rest day

## If Recovery Is Poor

Prioritize:

- More sleep
- More carbs around training
- Electrolytes
- 160–180 g protein daily
- Enough calories on rest day

# Recommended Starting Point

| Day Type | Calories |
|---|---:|
| Muay Thai / hard training days | ~3,700 kcal |
| Volleyball days | ~3,400 kcal |
| Rest day | ~2,900 kcal |

Daily targets:

- Protein: **165–175 g/day**
- Creatine: **3–5 g/day**
- Whey: **1 scoop most days**, **2 scoops on heavy or double-session days**

Track the following for 2 weeks:

- Morning bodyweight average
- Training performance
- Energy levels
- Sleep quality
- Muscle soreness
- Appetite

If bodyweight drops more than about **0.25–0.5 kg per week** and the goal is not fat loss, increase carbohydrates.
